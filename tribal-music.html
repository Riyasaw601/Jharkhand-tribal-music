Introduction

Jharkhand is famous for its rich tribal culture, traditional music, folk songs and dances. Music is an important part of the social and culture life of tribal comumnities.

Traditional music is performed during festivals, weddings,religious ceremonies, harvest celebrations and other social occasions. Singing, dancing and traditional musical instruments are often performed together.

Major Tribal Communities

  • Santhal
  • Munda
  • Oraon
  • Ho
  • Kharia
  • Birhor
  • Bhumij

Types of Tribal Music

Type of Music Desription
Festival Songs Traditional Songs performed during festivals.
Wedding Songs Songs performed during traditional marriage ceremonies
Dance Music Music used with traditional triabl dances.
Folk Songs Songs related to nature, culture and daily life.
Religious Songs Songs perfirmed during traditional ceremonies.

Tribal Dance and Music

Tribal music and dance are closely connected in jharkhand. People often perform traditional dances in groups while musician play drums and other instruments.

The rythm of traditional instruments helps dancers perform their steps together. Group singing and dancing also create a strong sense of commuinty participation.

Traditional Musical Instruments

Some important traditional instruments used in Jharkhand triabl music include Mandar, Nagara, Tamak, Dhol, Dhamsa, Bansuri, shehani and jhanj.

  • Mandar
  • Nagara
  • Tamak
  • Dhol
  • Dhamsa
  • Bansuri
  • Shehani
  • Jhanj

Music During Festivals

Muisc plays an important role during traditional festivals such as Sarhul, Karma, Sohrai, Tusu and Fagua. People celebrate these occasions with taditional songs, dances and musical instruments.

Importance of Tribal Music

  • It preserves tribal culture.
  • It protects traditional songs and dances.
  • It connects younger generations wiyh their heritage.
  • It brings community members together.
  • It is an imoportant part of festivals and celebrations.
  • It represents the cultural identity of jharkhand.
